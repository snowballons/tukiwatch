Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

1. What We Collect

TukiWatch stores your favorited channels, platform choices, and preferences locally on your device. It does not create an account and does not send this data to a TukiWatch server.

2. What We Don't Collect

We do not operate a TukiWatch backend and do not collect, store, or transmit your stream history, viewing habits, device identifiers, location data, or other personally identifiable information.

3. On-Device Processing

Stream resolution runs inside the app using Streamlink through Chaquopy. When you resolve or play a stream, your device connects directly to the relevant third-party platform or content delivery network. TukiWatch does not route those requests through a TukiWatch server.

4. Local Storage and Backups

Favorites and settings are stored in local SQLite and DataStore. Exporting a backup creates a JSON file in the location you choose through the Android Storage Access Framework. TukiWatch does not upload that file anywhere.

5. Third-Party Platforms

TukiWatch accesses public streaming data from platforms such as Twitch, YouTube, and Kick. Your interaction with those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies. TukiWatch does not operate accounts on those platforms; any optional credentials you supply are stored locally on your device.

6. Donations

Donations are optional and are handled by GitHub Sponsors or Buy Me a Coffee. TukiWatch does not receive or store your payment details. The privacy policies of those providers apply to their services.

7. Children's Privacy

TukiWatch is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of it, we will remove it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted here with a new effective date. Check back periodically.

9. Contact

Questions? Email snowballons@protonmail.com
