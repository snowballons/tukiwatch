Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 9, 2026

At StreamWatch, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in collecting only minimal data necessary for the app's functionality. When you use StreamWatch, we may collect:

  • Stream Preferences: Information about your favorited channels, platforms, and viewing preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is used to personalize your experience and provide relevant notifications.
  • User Settings: Your customized alert settings, theme preferences, and other in-app configurations are also stored locally on your device.
  • Anonymous Usage Data (Optional): We may collect anonymized, aggregated usage statistics to understand how the app is used, identify popular features, and improve performance. This data does not identify you personally. You will have the option to opt-out of this collection.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain the StreamWatch service.
  • Personalize your app experience, including stream recommendations and notifications.
  • Improve the functionality and performance of the app.
  • Communicate with you regarding updates or support (if you choose to contact us).

3. Data Storage and Security

Most of your personal preferences and stream data are stored locally on your device. We do not store your favorited streams or personal preferences on our servers unless explicitly stated for specific features (which will require your consent).

We are inspired by open-source principles and prioritize privacy. We employ reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of any information under our control. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This excludes trusted third parties who assist us in operating our application, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

We may also release your information when we believe release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce our site policies, or protect ours or others' rights, property, or safety.

5. Third-Party Links

Our app may contain links to third-party streaming platforms or services. These third-party sites have separate and independent privacy policies. We therefore have no responsibility or liability for the content and activities of these linked sites. Nonetheless, we seek to protect the integrity of our app and welcome any feedback about these sites.

6. Children's Privacy

StreamWatch is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under the age of 13 without verification of parental consent, we take steps to remove that information from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: streamwatch@snowballons.com
