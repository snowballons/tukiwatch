03 / About

Built for real viewers,
by real viewers

StreamWatch started as a personal fix. The existing options were bloated, full of ads, or required accounts just to follow a streamer. So we built something stripped back: an app that does exactly what you need and nothing you don't.

It's fully open-source under the MIT license. The API is public. There is no tracking, no monetisation plan, no venture capital. Just a tool that works.
