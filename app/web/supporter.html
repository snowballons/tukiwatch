Questions?

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your Lemon Squeezy dashboard at any time. Your supporter benefits continue until the end of your current billing period. No questions asked.

What happens if I miss a payment?

If a payment fails, your subscription moves to inactive status. You'll keep your benefits for the remainder of the billing cycle. After that, you drop back to free-tier rate limits. No penalties, no data loss.

How do I activate my supporter status?

After purchasing, Lemon Squeezy emails you a license key. Open TukiWatch, go to Settings > Backend Settings, and enter your license key. The API validates it and issues a session token. That's it.

Is there a one-time payment option?

Not currently. We're starting with a monthly tier because the server costs are ongoing. A lifetime tier may come later if demand exists — supporters will get first pick.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Nothing. Your favorites, sessions, and usage data are stored locally on your device. We don't track you. Canceling your support doesn't affect any data the app has already processed.

Is there a refund policy?

Refunds are handled by Lemon Squeezy. If you're not satisfied, contact them within 14 days of purchase. We trust our product — most supporters stick around because they find it useful.

Prefer a one-time gift?

You can also sponsor TukiWatch with a single payment. No recurring charge, no license key — just supporting the project.
